Acquisition engine

Target, reward, attribute — then reconcile the naira

Three systems working as one pipeline, all self-hosted and auditable like the rest of Agora.

Geo-targeted campaigns

Draw audiences down to LGA level on an open MapLibre map — no proprietary map tiles. Launch per-LGA offers, in the local language, and watch response rates by geography in real time.

Referral & commission engine

Per-sector bounties — ₦1,500 for a fintech agent referral, ₦5,000 for a rider — credited on verified activation, with nightly reconciliation against the ledger so every commission is accounted for.

Attribution that survives offline

UTM links for web, promo codes for SMS and voice, and QR codes for field agents and print — all resolved to a single CAC-by-channel view that refreshes in seconds.

Compliance by default

Growth that the NCC and NDPA can audit

Acquisition only works at scale if it stays legal. Agora Growth enforces Nigeria’s outreach rules in the send path itself — not in a policy document.

  • NCC DND 2442 suppression — do-not-disturb numbers filtered before every send
  • Quiet hours — no promotional calls or messages outside permitted windows
  • NDPA consent registry — auditable opt-in records with lawful-basis tracking
